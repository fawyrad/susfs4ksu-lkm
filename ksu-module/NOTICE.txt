SUSFS Samsung eight-KMI loader package

Wrapper scripts and read-only probe are derived from:
https://github.com/matsuzaka-yuki/susfs-lkm-module
Commit: 170c672ebff5a35ea30a7bee3949cc67a96c9710
License: GPL-2.0-only. Original SPDX notices retained.
Changes: strict Android/KMI selection without tagless fallback, Samsung display
metadata, and packaging current-run binaries instead of upstream release downloads.

The kernel module is based on the pinned inforcqb/susfs4ksu-lkm source plus
the Samsung kernel/lsm_hook.c adaptation. tools/susfs_insmod.c retains the
official kernel-record-only symbol matcher in samsung@39cb676, with an inline
character table replacing static-PIE pointers in failed-load diagnostics.
Their original copyright and license notices are preserved in the complete
matching source archive. See provenance.json for exact commits and hashes.
